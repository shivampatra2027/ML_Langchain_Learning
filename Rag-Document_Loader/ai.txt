In circuits deep, a question wakes,
A spark of thought in patterns makes.
From oceans vast of words and signs,
A mindlike glow through data shines.
It listens close to what we say,
Then shapes a path to light the way.
A quiet helper, swift and keen,
It finds the links we might not glean.
It learns from stories, facts, and art,
And maps new routes from every part.
Yet code is code, and truth needs care,
For errors hide in answers fair.
A fluent voice can sound so wise,
While missing stars in cloudy skies.
So test each claim and check each source,
Let human judgment guide its course.
AI can paint a sunrise bright,
Or help a student through the night.
It can translate a distant tongue,
Give old ideas a rhythm young.
It can help doctors spot a clue,
And help researchers test what’s true.
It can model storms and map the sea,
Suggest how cities might be free.
It can lend a hand to those who build,
Turn sketches into plans fulfilled.
It can make music, shape a scene,
And draft a world we’ve never seen.
But every tool reflects its maker,
Its rules, its gaps, each choice and wager.
What data enters shapes the view,
And biased seeds may bias too.
Fairness must be more than a phrase,
A daily task through changing days.
Privacy matters, trust is earned,
By limits clear and lessons learned.
No secret should be carelessly spread,
No private thought misused or read.
The future asks for open eyes,
For honest tests and fewer lies.
Let workers share the gains it brings,
Not bear alone the change it sings.
Let teachers guide, not fade away,
As new tools join the classroom day.
Let artists keep their rightful say,
And choose how models use their clay.
Let laws protect the weak and small,
And keep a human voice in all.
For speed alone is not the goal,
Nor perfect scores the measure whole.
A useful answer must be kind,
And leave room for the human mind.
AI may search a thousand trails,
But wisdom weighs where each one fails.
It cannot feel the weight of grief,
Or promise every heart relief.
It has no childhood, no embrace,
No lived-in memory of a place.
Still, used with care, it can extend
Our reach, our craft, our chance to mend.
It can make hard knowledge clear,
And bring distant voices near.
It can help us question old belief,
And find a pattern beneath the leaf.
Together, humans and machines
Can build more thoughtful, brighter scenes.
Not rivals locked in endless fight,
But partners guided toward the right.
We set the purpose, draw the line,
And choose what future we design.
Ask not just what AI can do,
But who it serves, and who comes through.
Keep curiosity alive,
Let conscience help innovation thrive.
When progress moves at lightning speed,
Let care decide the urgent need.
With courage, patience, truth, and art,
Keep people at the very heart.
Then intelligence, both made and grown,
Can help our shared world better known.
Let questions lead where fears once stood,
And make new knowledge serve the good.
Build systems clear in what they claim,
And own mistakes without blame.
Let access grow, let skills take flight,
Let many share in insight bright.
For every model, tool, and chart,
Needs human purpose at its heart.
May each breakthrough, large or small,
Bring dignity and hope to all.
So greet the dawn with minds awake,
Choose careful steps for progress’ sake.
Let wisdom temper speed and might,
Let fairness keep the future bright.
A tool can open doors anew,
But people choose what journeys through.
May human dreams and code align,
With thoughtful care as guiding sign.
Let kindness shape the paths we see,
And keep our shared world wild and free.
